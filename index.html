What It Measures

Three angles on every song

01

Word & line stats

Total words, unique words, average words per line, and roughly how long the song takes to read out loud.

02

Mood & tone

Lyrics are scored against word lists for love, heartbreak, joy, and anger to estimate the song's overall emotional lean.

03

Repetition & hooks

The most repeated word or phrase is usually the hook — the analyzer surfaces it along with how often it repeats.

How The Analyzer Works

Lyrics in, insight out

Step 1

Paste or listen

Any verse, chorus, or full song — or speak lyrics into the mic for identification.

Step 2

Text gets parsed

Python-style logic strips punctuation, splits into words and lines, and counts frequency.

Step 3

Mood is scored

Each word is checked against emotion word lists to tally an overall tone.

Step 4

Match & results

Word stats, mood, likely hook, and best library match appear instantly.

What's Under The Hood

Built with Python + browser APIs

The analysis logic is rule-based text processing; the Shazam-style UI uses the Web Speech API (when available) and a small in-browser song library for matching.

Text parsing

Lyrics are split into lines and words, punctuation stripped, so every metric is counted cleanly.

Mood scoring

Word lists for love, heartbreak, joy, and anger are tallied against the lyrics to estimate overall tone.

Repetition detection

Word and line frequency is tracked to surface the most repeated hook in the song.

Song matching

Input lyrics (or speech transcript) are scored against a curated library of OVO / R&B tracks by shared significant words.

Listen UI

Microphone access + Web Speech Recognition powers the Shazam-style button; pure audio fingerprinting would need a backend service.
